Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What Data We Collect

When you use ResumeFit AI, you submit two things: your resume file (PDF, DOCX, or TXT, up to 5 MB) and a job description you paste into the form. That's it.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information. There are no user accounts and no registration. You can use the tool completely anonymously.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your resume and job description are used for one purpose only: to generate your analysis results. The text is passed through our local NLP pipeline — text extraction, keyword comparison, and semantic similarity scoring — and the results are returned to your browser.

Your data is never used to retrain or fine-tune any AI model. It is never read by a human. It is never repurposed beyond the single analysis you requested.

3. Data Retention

We retain nothing. Your uploaded file and its extracted text exist only in server memory for the duration of a single request — typically a few seconds. As soon as the analysis is complete and the response is sent, the data is discarded.

There is no database. There are no log files containing your resume text. There is no backup of your submission anywhere. Once the request ends, it is gone.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any form of tracking. We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or any similar service.

Flask, the web framework powering this app, may set a minimal session cookie to handle the request lifecycle. This cookie contains no personal information, is not shared with anyone, and expires when you close your browser tab.

5. Third-Party Services

None. Every part of the analysis pipeline runs on our own server. The AI model (SBERT all-MiniLM-L6-v2) is loaded locally — no data is sent to OpenAI, Hugging Face, or any external API.

The only external resource this page loads is the Google Fonts stylesheet for typography. That request contains no information about you or your resume.

6. Children's Privacy

ResumeFit AI is a tool for analyzing professional resumes and job descriptions. It is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are under 13, please do not use this service.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make any meaningful changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and push the change to our public GitHub repository. Because we collect no contact information, we cannot notify you directly — checking this page or watching the repository is the best way to stay informed.

8. Contact

ResumeFit AI is a university course project built by Team PhaseShift. It is not a commercial product and is provided as-is for educational purposes.

If you have questions or concerns about this policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
