Architecture & Workflow

01

Upload Source File

Add your resume in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format. Our parser extracts the raw text. For highest fidelity, ensure your PDF is text-based rather than a scanned image.

02

Input Target Context

Paste the complete job requisition. The system uses this as the baseline context to measure your resume against. Higher word counts yield more precise telemetry.

03

Vector Analysis

We utilize semantic vector embeddings to compare your document against the requisition. This measures contextual meaning and conceptual alignment, not just exact keyword matches.

04

Execute Commits

Review the IDE Canvas. We highlight exact locations in the target description where your resume is lacking, and generate a checklist of actionable commits to improve your match signal.

Understanding Telemetry Scores

80–100%

High Signal

Strong algorithmic alignment. Minor optimizations recommended. Ready for submission.

60–79%

Moderate Signal

Solid baseline. Executing the suggested commits will significantly improve ATS survivability.

40–59%

Weak Signal

Noticeable context gaps. Structural resume updates and skill gap closures are required.

< 40%

Critical Variance

Mismatch detected. Reassess if this requisition aligns with your current documented experience.
